Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what Zukrit stores about you, why, and who can see it. The data controller is Zukrit, Denmark. Contact: zukrit@proton.me.

What we store

WhatWhy
Your login: an ID from Google (and, in test versions only, a phone number)So you can log in. We don’t get your Google password.
Your profile: first name, date of birth, photo, and what you choose to write (about you, job, neighbourhood, interests)So hosts and guests know who they meet, and to check that you are over 18 and fit an event’s age range.
Your location, when you allow itTo show events near you. Others never see your location.
Events you host: title, description, place, exact address, cover photo, age range and your question to guestsTo show your event and let people ask to join.
Requests, greetings, answers, group chat messages and messages when you give up a spotSo hosts and guests can find each other and talk.
Who you follow, block, skip and reportTo show you the right events, keep blocked people away from you, and handle reports.

We don’t use your data for advertising, we don’t sell it, and we don’t use tracking or analytics tools in the app.

Who can see what

  • Your first name, age (unless you hide it), photo and profile are shown to hosts when you ask to join, and to guests when you host.
  • The exact address of an event is only shown to the host and to guests the host has said yes to.
  • Distances are measured from a rough position (about 600 metres), never from the exact address.
  • Group chats are only shown to the host and the guests with a spot.
  • Reports are only seen by us. The person reported is never told who reported them.

Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you have signed up for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)). We handle reports, blocks and misuse because we have a legitimate interest in keeping users safe (article 6(1)(f)).

Who helps us

  • Google Firebase handles login. Google may process data outside the EU under the EU’s standard contractual clauses.
  • Contabo runs our server and database in the EU.
  • Cloudflare R2 stores photos and backups in the EU.

They only process data on our behalf and may not use it for anything else.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, interests, messages and requests straight away, and cancel the events you host. Your guests are told. Reports you made or that were made about you are kept without your name, so we can still see patterns of misuse. Backups are deleted after 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and change most of your data in the app, and delete your account under Settings → Delete account (how). You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to stop processing it, by writing to zukrit@proton.me. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we tell you in the app first.
