Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open Zukrit and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Tick that you understand, and tap Delete.

Without the app

Write to zukrit@proton.me from the e-mail you log in with, and ask us to delete your account. We do it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, photos, interests, requests and messages are deleted straight away. Events you host are cancelled, and your guests are told. You are removed from events you were going to. Reports are kept without your name. Backups are deleted after 30 days. Read more in our privacy policy.
